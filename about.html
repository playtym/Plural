About Plural

We're building India's AI generation.

India gave the world zero. Algebra. Chess. The decimal system. For millennia, India has been a source of foundational ideas that changed civilisation. We believe the next wave of those ideas will come from a 14-year-old in a small town in Rajasthan, a 16-year-old in Guwahati, a 12-year-old in Kochi — children who have something powerful to build, and who just need the skills and the community to build it.

Plural exists to give them those skills.

We are India's AI-first coding education platform for children aged 8–18. We teach real AI and programming — Python, LLMs, machine learning, APIs — through live online classes in tiny batches of 8 students. Every session builds something real. Every student graduates with a portfolio, not just a certificate.

Our mission

Every Indian child — regardless of which city they're in, which school they attend, or how much money their family has — deserves to be a builder of AI, not just a user of it.

The world is being reshaped by artificial intelligence. The children who understand how to build with it will have enormous power to create, to solve problems, and to lead. The children who don't will be left behind. We refuse to let geography or economics be the deciding factor.

Why we're different

🤖

AI-first, not AI-added

We didn't add an "AI module" to a Scratch curriculum. We built an AI-native curriculum from the ground up — designed for the world that exists today, not 2015.

👥

8 students max. Always.

This is the most important rule at Plural. A mentor who knows each child's name, project, and roadblocks is not a luxury — it's the product.

🚀

Projects over certificates

Every session builds something real. Students graduate with deployed AI tools on GitHub — work they're genuinely proud of, not a PDF with their name on it.

🇮🇳

Built for India

22 Indian languages. Indian problem sets. Indian mentors. We don't import Western curricula — we build for the context our students actually live in.

Our programs

We offer three age-appropriate tracks that grow with your child:

  • Little Makers (ages 8–10) — Robotics, block coding, logic puzzles. The first spark.
  • Code Explorers (ages 10–13) — JavaScript, Python, and web development. From user to maker.
  • AI Builders (ages 13–18) — LLMs, machine learning, real APIs. Portfolio-worthy by 16.
