Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2025 · Last updated: 29 May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Plural Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Plural", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at plural.ai and our educational services.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Register for a batch or trial session (child's name, age, parent name, email address, WhatsApp number)
  • Fill in any contact or enquiry form on our website
  • Communicate with us by email or WhatsApp

We also collect limited usage data automatically (browser type, pages visited, session duration) through standard server logs and analytics tools. We do not use invasive tracking.

2. How we use your information

  • To process enrolments and schedule live sessions
  • To send you session reminders, Zoom links, and batch group invites via WhatsApp
  • To send weekly progress updates to parents
  • To respond to your enquiries
  • To improve our programs and website

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Children's data

Our services are designed for children aged 8–18. We collect only the minimum information necessary (child's first name and age) to provide our educational services. Parents or guardians provide consent on behalf of their children at the time of registration. We do not directly market to children.

4. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you have an active relationship with Plural, and for a reasonable period thereafter for operational and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@plural.ai.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. Live sessions are conducted over Zoom with waiting-room controls enabled. Participant data is never publicly shared.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@plural.ai.
